EXECUTIVE SUMMARY  OLIST E-COMMERCE ANALYSIS
============================================================

1. Executive Overview
The business is operating from a healthy revenue base, but delivery execution and customer retention remain the largest blockers to sustainable growth. Revenue momentum is positive, although volatility from seasonal promotions, cancellations, and late deliveries creates margin and service risk.

2. Business Performance
- Total revenue: R$15.4M across 99,441 orders
- On-time delivery: 91.89%
- Average order value: R$160
- Peak monthly growth: +53.5% in November 2017
- Average review score: 4.09/5

3. Critical Issues
- 8.11% late delivery rate, peaking at 23.9% in the worst state
- 29.0% of customers are lost or inactive
- 1.24% cancellation rate equates to R$269,735 in lost revenue

4. Strategic Opportunities
- Win-back campaigns for lost customers could unlock R$1.25M
- Champion retention and loyalty offers should improve repeat purchase rates
- Delivery improvements in high-weight/late-state routes could materially reduce churn

5. Recommended 90-Day Action Plan
- Fix fulfillment bottlenecks in the worst-performing states
- Launch targeted retention campaigns for at-risk and lost customer segments
- Rebalance marketing around peak-demand periods to protect margin and conversion

6. Conclusion
The company has a solid revenue foundation and strong demand visibility, but it must reduce execution friction in logistics and retention to unlock sustained profitable growth.
